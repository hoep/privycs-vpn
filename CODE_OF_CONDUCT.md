# Code of Conduct

The Privycs VPN project wants every participant — in issues, pull
requests, and any other project space — to have a welcoming and
respectful experience.

## Our standard

Be respectful and constructive. Assume good faith. Disagree on the
technical merits, never on the person. Conduct that makes the project
an unwelcoming or unsafe space for others is not acceptable.

This project adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**
as its full code of conduct. Please read it — it describes the
expected behaviour and the enforcement process in detail.

## Reporting

To report a concern, contact the maintainers at **dev@privycs.com**.
Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers will review every
report and may take any action they consider appropriate, including a
temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.
