v1.1.5.33 (build 129) — tvOS: a real, reachable Connect button.

Custom focusable buttons (the disc) wouldn't reliably take focus on tvOS. So the
disc is now just a status visual, and the connect/disconnect action is a STANDARD
borderedProminent button right beneath it — the exact button type you already reach
for Refresh/Settings. So you can finally focus it with the remote and connect.

HOW TO TEST (Apple TV)
- Focus the "Connect" button (below the disc) with the remote → press → it connects.
  Then check internet (kill switch is off by default), and the kill-switch toggle in
  Settings.
