v1.1.4.1 (build 95) — in-app language now applies everywhere.

Fixes strings that ignored your in-app language choice and stayed in the device
language: the Connect-screen picker ("Connections"/"Pools"), the detail rows
("Endpoint", "Last handshake"), relative times ("1 minute ago"), and the
tunnel-health pill ("Healthy"). These used String(localized:), which bypasses
the in-app language override (only Text/NSLocalizedString went through it). All
~111 such strings now resolve against the chosen language.

HOW TO TEST
- Set the app language (Settings ▸ Appearance ▸ Language) to one different from
  your device language.
- Connect screen: the protocol picker tabs, Endpoint / Last handshake labels,
  the "… ago" time, and the health pill should all be in the CHOSEN language —
  not your device language.
- Switch language again → everything updates without a restart.

REGRESSION CHECK
- All other screens still localize correctly; widget protocol icons; IPSec
  counter; delete cleanup.
