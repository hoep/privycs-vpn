v1.1.5.48 (build 144) — tvOS: Settings & Rules rebuilt to the design template.

The Settings/Rules screens were a 10-foot usability mess — raw tvOS pickers, system
toggles and bare text fields. Rebuilt them on the design template's component
vocabulary:
- Setting rows now follow the template's "setrow": bold title + muted description on
  the left, control on the right, full-width focusable cards with the teal focus glow.
- Custom toggle pills (the template's .tg) instead of system toggles.
- Segmented controls (.seg) for Health mode / interval / threshold and Language —
  big tappable segments, selected one in teal — instead of pop-up pickers.
- DNS: a mono field + a horizontal row of preset chips (Cloudflare, Quad9, …).
- Backup & Restore and Unlink are proper setrows with .setb-style action buttons.
- Rules screen uses the same toggle-pill row + setting blocks.

HOW TO TEST (Apple TV): open Settings and Rules. Everything should look consistent
with the Connect/Configs design — large rows, teal focus, no pop-up pickers. Move
focus through every control with the remote and confirm it's all reachable + legible.
Send a screenshot of Settings and Rules.
