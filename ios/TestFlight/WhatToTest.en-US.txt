v1.1.5.7 (build 103) — widget switch: stop on-demand fighting the switch.

build 102 logged "WireGuard starting…" right after tapping OpenVPN — that was
iOS ON-DEMAND restarting the OLD protocol while the widget started the new one.

keeps on-demand off for the manual switch, so the old protocol can't come back.
Still fully in-place — the app is NOT opened.
