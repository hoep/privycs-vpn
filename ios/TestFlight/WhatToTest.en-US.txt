v1.1.5.55 (build 151) — tvOS: fix pool rotation + exit-point after relaunch/upgrade
+ brand slogan.

FIX (pool after upgrade/relaunch): when the app restarted and the OS kept the pool
tunnel up (always-on), the app didn't restart the rotation timer and didn't know the
current server, so:
- the rotation countdown stayed put / didn't run, and
- the Connect pool card showed no exit point (server).
Now on launch the app restores the active pool's current member (exit point shows)
and re-arms the rotation timer (recomputing a stale interval so it doesn't rotate the
instant you open the app). Always-on auto-connect now also covers pools.

Also: the slogan under the Privycs logo now reads "Secure.Private.Simple." (was "VPN").

HOW TO TEST: connect a rotating pool, force-quit/relaunch (or take the TestFlight
update) → reopen → the Connect card should show the current server and the rotation
countdown should be ticking again.
