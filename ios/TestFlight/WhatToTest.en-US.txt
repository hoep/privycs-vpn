v1.1.5.34 (build 130) — tvOS: Connect button moved into the top bar (reachable).

You can reach the top-bar buttons (Refresh/Settings) but not centre-of-screen
controls — that's a tvOS focus reality. So Connect/Disconnect is now the FIRST
button in the top bar, right next to Refresh and Settings. The disc below is just a
status visual.

HOW TO TEST (Apple TV)
- Top bar: "Connect" (teal, leftmost action) → focus it with the remote → press →

  Settings.
