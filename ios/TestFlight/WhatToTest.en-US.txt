v1.1.5.15 (build 111) — widget traffic counter now advances every second.

build 110 projected the counter every 10s; this emits a timeline entry PER SECOND
(120 entries / 2 min) projecting from the tunnel's live speed, so the widget
counter steps every second on its own — pre-computed entries cost no iOS refresh
budget. Each real refresh (~60s) resyncs to the true value.
(iOS may coalesce view updates under low power, but it's now as smooth as a widget
can be; the app remains exact per-second.)
