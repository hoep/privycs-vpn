v1.1.5.35 (build 131) — tvOS: visible top-bar buttons + kill switch removed.

- Top-bar buttons (Connect/Refresh/Settings) were white-on-white when not focused
  (borderedProminent renders that way unfocused on tvOS). Switched to the .card
  style (always-visible surface, like the server cards) with explicit label colours
  — Connect is teal so it stands out; all stay readable focused or not.
- KILL SWITCH removed on tvOS: it only forces IPv6 through the tunnel, which tvOS
  can't carry, so it blackholed v6 and killed internet/DNS. It's now hard-off and
  the toggle is gone from Settings. (Connecting works + has internet by default.)

HOW TO TEST (Apple TV)
- Top bar: Connect is visible (teal) even unfocused → connect → you have internet.
- Settings no longer shows a kill switch (DNS + crash reports remain).
