v1.1.5.11 (build 107) — widget switch: protocol display syncs for WG/AmneziaWG too.

Switching FROM IPSec synced correctly, but switching between PTP protocols
(WireGuard ↔ AmneziaWG ↔ OpenVPN) kept showing the OLD protocol: the previous
PTP tunnel's live stats lingered with the old protocol id and overrode the
display until the new tunnel wrote its own. The widget now also stamps the new
protocol onto those live stats right after the switch, so the active-protocol
pill updates immediately for all protocols (IPSec was always the baseline here).

HOW TO TEST
- From the widget, switch WireGuard ↔ AmneziaWG ↔ OpenVPN ↔ IPSec in any order →
  the highlighted/active protocol updates immediately every time, no app needed.
- Open the app after a switch → the active protocol matches.
