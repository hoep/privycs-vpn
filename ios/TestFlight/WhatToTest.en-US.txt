v1.1.5.57 (build 153) — tvOS mini-server page: dark/light skin + prominent file upload.

The page the Apple TV serves (scan its QR → opens in your phone's browser) is
redesigned:
- Follows your phone browser's Dark/Light mode (prefers-color-scheme) — Privycs
  colors in both, with the brand + "Secure.Private.Simple." header.
- "Upload a file" is now the primary action and accepts any locally-saved file
  (Files / iCloud Drive): .zip → a server pool, .conf → a WireGuard/AmneziaWG
  connection, .ovpn / .sswan are accepted (and reported as not runnable on tvOS,
  which only does WireGuard/AmneziaWG). Pasting config/backup text is still there as
  a secondary card.

HOW TO TEST: Apple TV → Configs → Add/restore (or Settings → Backup & Restore) → scan
the QR with your phone → the page should match your browser's light/dark mode → tap
"Upload a file", pick a .zip/.conf from Files → it imports on the TV.
