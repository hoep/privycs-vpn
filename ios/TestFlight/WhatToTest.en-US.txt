v1.1.5.12 (build 108) — widget traffic counters: reset on switch + periodic refresh.

On a protocol switch the widget now resets the traffic counters/graph to 0 (new
tunnel session) instead of leaving the previous protocol's numbers, and the tunnel
nudges the widget to re-read live stats every ~30s.

NOTE (iOS limitation): a home-screen widget cannot show per-second traffic — iOS
throttles widget refreshes (typically minutes, not seconds, when the app is
closed). So the counter steps up periodically, not live. Open the app for live
per-second numbers.

HOW TO TEST
- Switch protocols from the widget → the counter resets to 0 and then climbs as
  traffic flows (updates every ~30s+, subject to iOS throttling), no stale value
  from the previous protocol.
- Leave the widget connected a few minutes → rx/tx should advance.

REGRESSION: in-app live counter still per-second; switch sync (build 107) intact.
