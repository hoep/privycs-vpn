v1.1.5.9 (build 105) — widget switch: instant pill sync.

The in-place protocol switch works (build 104). This build fixes the display: the
widget now writes the new active protocol into its own snapshot right after a
switch, so the active-protocol pill highlights immediately — no need to open the
app to see the change.

HOW TO TEST
- Widget: switch WG ↔ OpenVPN (↔ IPSec) → the highlighted pill updates instantly,
  without opening the app.
- IPSec specifically: tap the IPSec pill → it should switch to IPSec (NOT
  WireGuard). If it goes to WireGuard, open the app → Settings → Logs and send the
  "widget switch[ipsec]:" line (shows started/FAILED + the connection status).

REGRESSION CHECK
- In-app connect/switch all protocols; pools; on-demand; OpenVPN counter.
