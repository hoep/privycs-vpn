v1.1.5.49 / iOS 1.1.5.33 (build 145) — tvOS Theme switch + share a Pool to Apple TV.

tvOS:
- Settings → Theme: System / Dark / Light (segmented). Flips the whole palette
  immediately (TVColor is adaptive).

iPhone → Apple TV (Settings → Backup → Send to Apple TV):
- New "A pool" option. Pick a pool and its servers are pushed to the Apple TV as
  individual connections (one per WireGuard/AmneziaWG member; OpenVPN/IPSec members
  are skipped since tvOS can't run them). They appear under "Imported" on the TV.
- Still supports sending a single connection or a full encrypted backup.

HOW TO TEST:
1. tvOS Settings → Theme → switch Dark/Light/System; the UI should re-theme live.
2. iPhone: Settings → Backup → Send to Apple TV → scan the TV's QR (Configs →
   Add / restore, or Settings → Backup & Restore) → choose "A pool" → send. Confirm
   each pool server shows up under Imported on the TV and connects.
