v1.1.3.19 (build 88) — one VPN at a time + IPSec profile switching.

Fixes two related issues:
- The app could start TWO VPNs at once (IPSec AND WireGuard) — a stale
  on-demand-armed WireGuard profile fired while IPSec came up.
- Switching to a DIFFERENT IPSec profile didn't take effect (the still-armed
  WireGuard raced the IPSec slot).

WHAT CHANGED
- Before bringing up a tunnel, the app now DISABLES every other VPN profile
  (the IPSec personal VPN when starting WireGuard/AmneziaWG/OpenVPN, and all
  packet-tunnel profiles when starting IPSec) → exactly one VPN active.

HOW TO TEST
1. Connection with both IPSec and WireGuard configs: connect IPSec → in
   iOS Settings ▸ VPN only the IPSec/personal VPN should be up (no WireGuard
   starting alongside).
2. Switch between two IPSec profiles (e.g. ipsShield1 → ipsShield2): the new
   one should take over the single IPSec slot and connect.
3. Switch IPSec ↔ WireGuard back and forth — only one runs at a time.

REGRESSION CHECK
- WG/AWG/OVPN connect/disconnect, pools, on-demand rules, traffic counter.
