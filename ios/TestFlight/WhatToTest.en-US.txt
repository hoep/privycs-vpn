v1.1.5.8 (build 104) — NEW: per-protocol managers (in-place widget switch, incl. IPSec).

Big change: the app now keeps a SEPARATE, ready-to-start VPN profile per protocol
(WG/AWG/OpenVPN each + the IPSec slot) instead of reconfiguring one. The widget
can then switch by just stop+start — no reconfigure (which the widget can't do).
This is why earlier widget switches failed. iOS Settings ▸ VPN will now show one
entry per protocol of a connection — that's expected.

   EACH protocol (WireGuard, AmneziaWG, OpenVPN, IPSec), switch protocols via the
   in-app pills, check pools + on-demand. Everything must still work as before.

B) WIDGET SWITCH: first connect each protocol you want to switch between ONCE in
   the app (this creates its profile). THEN from the widget tap a protocol pill →
   it switches IN PLACE (no app opens), incl. IPSec. The active protocol on the
   widget + Settings ▸ VPN should change.
   (If a pill does nothing, the log says "manager … not found — connect it once
   in-app first" — connect that protocol in-app once, then retry.)
