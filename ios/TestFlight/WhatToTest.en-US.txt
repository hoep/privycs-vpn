v1.1.3.7 (build 79) — New Privycs design system: colors + typography.

This build rolls the new Privycs design system across the whole app
(matching Desktop + Android). Please look at the LOOK & FEEL on top of
normal functionality.

1. NEW VISUAL THEME
   • Dark mode: deeper, teal-tinted "command-console" background with
     hairline borders (was a flatter blue-grey). Light mode: clean white
     with a faint teal tint.
   • Teal accents on light backgrounds are now a darker teal so text and
     icons stay readable (they used to be too light on white).
   • Switch Settings → Theme between System / Dark / Light and confirm both
     modes look right and text is legible everywhere.

2. NEW FONTS
   • The app now ships Inter (UI text) and Fira Code (for numbers/labels:
     uptime, transfer counters, IP addresses, logs, config text).
   • Check that text renders correctly — no missing glyphs, no clipped or
     overlapping labels — on the Connect, Configs, Pool detail, Logs and
     Settings screens.

3. REGRESSION CHECK (should be unchanged)
   • Connect/disconnect over WireGuard, AmneziaWG, OpenVPN and IPSec.
   • VPN pools: import a .zip, swipe-left to delete, pool-only connect.
   • Smart Decision Engine panel (Settings → Automatic protocol selection).
   • On older devices (iOS 15 / older iPads) everything should still work.
