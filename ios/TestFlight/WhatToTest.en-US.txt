v1.1.3.10 (build 81) — Live traffic counter after auto-reconnect.

Fixes a bug where the live traffic counter (download/upload bytes + speed
graph) froze after the VPN reconnected on its own.

HOW TO TEST
1. Settings: turn ON the auto-tunnel / Network Rules, with a rule that connects
   on your current network. Connect (WireGuard, AmneziaWG or OpenVPN).
2. Tap Disconnect manually. The app should auto-reconnect within a few seconds
   (on-demand).
3. Confirm the download/upload counters and the speed graph KEEP UPDATING after
   that automatic reconnect — they should no longer freeze. (Previously you had
   to kill and relaunch the app to get live numbers back.)
4. Repeat over WireGuard, AmneziaWG and OpenVPN.

NOTE: IPSec/IKEv2 shows 0 B for traffic by design — Apple's built-in IKEv2 does
not expose byte counters. That is unchanged and unrelated to this fix.

REGRESSION CHECK
- Normal connect/disconnect; counter live during a normal session.
- Pools and single connections behave as before.
