v1.1.5.36 (build 132) — tvOS: Auto-connect / Always-on (autostart + on-demand).

New Settings toggle "Connect automatically (Always-on)". When on, the VPN profile
gets an on-demand connect rule, so the OS auto-connects and KEEPS the tunnel up on
ANY network (WiFi or Ethernet), even after a reboot and without opening the app —
that's autostart + on-demand in one. The app also connects on launch when it's on.
Turning it off (or pressing Disconnect) disarms it so it stays disconnected.

HOW TO TEST (Apple TV)
- Settings → enable "Connect automatically" → it connects and stays up; reboot the
  Apple TV and it should reconnect on its own. Disconnect/disable to stop.
- Works on WiFi and Ethernet.
