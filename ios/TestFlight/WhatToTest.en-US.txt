v1.1.5.46 / iOS 1.1.5.32 (build 142) — Apple TV: manual configs + backup/restore without the gateway, and "Send to Apple TV" on iPhone.

Apple TV has no Files app, document picker, or camera, so configs/backups now arrive
over the LOCAL NETWORK — no gateway, no cloud, only the Local Network permission:

tvOS:
- Configs → "Add / restore" shows a QR + PIN. The Apple TV hosts a tiny local-network
  endpoint. Scan the QR with ANY phone camera to open a paste/upload page in the
  browser, paste a .conf (or a backup file + passphrase), and send.
- Imported connections appear in their own list, are selectable, and connect like
  gateway configs. tvOS runs WireGuard + AmneziaWG only — OpenVPN/IPSec are rejected
  with a clear message. Backup restore uses the same cross-platform encrypted format.

iPhone:
- Settings → Backup → "Send to Apple TV": scan the code shown on the TV, pick a
  connection or an encrypted backup, and push it straight to the TV over Wi-Fi.

HOW TO TEST:
1. tvOS: Configs → Add / restore → scan the QR with your phone → paste a WireGuard
   .conf → it appears in "Imported" and connects.
2. iPhone: Settings → Backup → Send to Apple TV → scan → send a connection. Confirm
   it shows up on the TV.
3. Backup: send/paste a .pvcbackup with its passphrase to the TV → connections restore.
   Send a screenshot if the QR doesn't render or the Local Network prompt misbehaves.
