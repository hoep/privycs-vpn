v1.1.4 (build 94) — home-screen widget protocol icons.

The widget's connect button and the protocol-switch pills used generic system
symbols before. They now show the REAL WireGuard / AmneziaWG / OpenVPN / IPSec
brand logos (template-tinted — white when connected, grey when idle), matching
the in-app Connect button and the Android widget. Two remaining pool-import
hints are now localized into all six languages too.

HOW TO TEST
- Add the home-screen widget. Connect each protocol → the disc shows THAT
  protocol's logo (not a generic system glyph), white while connected.
- The protocol-switch pills show the matching per-protocol logos.
- Disconnected: the logo is grey.

REGRESSION CHECK
- Widget connect/disconnect + protocol switch work; traffic + sparkline; IPSec
  counter on profile switch; connection-delete cleanup.
