v1.1.3.20 (build 89) — IPSec traffic counter + really stop the other VPN.

Two fixes from a device log:
- IPSec traffic counter stayed 0. The counter watched for a "utun" interface,
  but Apple's IKEv2 personal VPN brings up an "ipsec0" interface instead — so
  the tunnel was never found. Now it watches ipsec* too → real rx/tx.
- When starting one VPN, the other (e.g. an on-demand WireGuard) could pop back
  up and fight it. We now disarm on-demand + disable + SAVE before stopping it,
  so it can't auto-restart on the route change.

HOW TO TEST
1. Connect IPSec → the traffic counter should climb (not stuck at 0) while you
   browse.
2. Connect IPSec → in iOS Settings ▸ VPN exactly ONE VPN is active and stays
   that way (no WireGuard creeping back), and connectivity works.
3. Switch IPSec ↔ WireGuard and between two IPSec profiles — one at a time.

REGRESSION CHECK
- WG/AWG/OVPN counters + connect/disconnect, pools, on-demand rules.
