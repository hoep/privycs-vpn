v1.1.5.32 (build 128) — tvOS: Connect disc fully navigable (focus re-entry fixed).

The disc could be reached on launch but never again once you moved away — the
focusScope/prefersDefaultFocus I used captured initial focus and blocked re-entry.
Removed both; the disc is now a normal focusable .card in a focus section, so the
remote can move onto it, away, and BACK any number of times.

(First build with the new split CI — this is tvOS-only; the iOS app is not rebuilt.)

HOW TO TEST (Apple TV)
- Move focus onto the Connect disc, away to a server card / the top bar, and BACK
  onto the disc — repeatedly. Press to connect/disconnect. Then: WireGuard → internet?
