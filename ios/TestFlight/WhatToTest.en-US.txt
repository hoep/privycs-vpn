v1.1.5.37 (build 133) — tvOS: WiFi-specific auto-connect (SSID on-demand rules).

In Settings under "Auto-connect on these WiFi networks" you can now add WiFi names
(SSIDs). When Always-on is on AND the list is non-empty, the VPN connects ONLY on
those networks and disconnects elsewhere (the OS evaluates the SSID — no SSID read
needed by the app). Empty list = connect on any network (WiFi or Ethernet).

HOW TO TEST (Apple TV)
- Settings → enable "Connect automatically" → add your home WiFi name exactly →
  the TV connects on that WiFi and not on others. Remove the entry to go back to
  any-network.
NOTE: if SSID matching isn't supported by tvOS the build will flag it and we'll
keep always-on only.
