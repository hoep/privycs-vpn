v1.1.3.18 (build 87) — App ↔ iOS-VPN profile sync.

On launch the app now reflects the VPN that's ACTUALLY running in iOS, so its
selected/active connection matches reality (e.g. a tunnel iOS brought up on its
own, or one left running from a previous session). One-shot at launch — it never
overrides a live switch you make in the app.

HOW TO TEST
- With a VPN already connected, force-quit and relaunch the app: the Connect
  screen should show the correct connection as the active/selected one (not a
  different/default one).
- Connect/disconnect/switch normally afterwards — behaviour unchanged.

REGRESSION CHECK
- IPSec stays connected (v1.1.3.16 fix), traffic counter, WG/AWG/OVPN, pools,
  on-demand rules all unchanged.
