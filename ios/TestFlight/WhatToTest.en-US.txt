v1.1.5.29 (build 125) — tvOS: Connect disc focusable, white button text, real toggles.

- The Connect disc now uses the focusable .card button style (.plain isn't reliably
  focusable on tvOS) — the remote can finally land on it to connect/disconnect.
- Top-bar Refresh/Settings buttons: white text on teal in BOTH light and dark (the
  app's global text colour was overriding the prominent button label — fixed).
- Settings rebuilt as a standard Form: Kill Switch + Anonymous crash reports are now
  native tvOS toggles (focusable switches), DNS field, Unlink, Gateway + Version.

HOW TO TEST (Apple TV)
- Move the remote onto the Connect disc → it highlights/lifts → press to connect.
- Light mode: Refresh/Settings text is white + readable.
- Settings shows real toggle switches you can flip.
