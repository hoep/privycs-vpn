v1.1.5.27 (build 123) — tvOS: kill-switch internet fix + wide no-scroll layout.

LIKELY THE NO-INTERNET FIX: the kill switch defaulted ON, and its IPv6 ::/0
injection forces all v6 into the tunnel; on tvOS the v6 data plane is unreliable,
so that blackholes IPv6 → DNS/connections fail (Apple TV prefers v6). The kill
switch now defaults OFF on tvOS (re-enable in Settings if your gateway does v6).

Layout: when connected the Connect disc is CENTERED with the traffic counters on
the LEFT and connection details (VPN IP / Endpoint / Last handshake) on the RIGHT —
no more scrolling. Server cards now show the protocol's brand logo in its colour
(WG red / OpenVPN orange / AmneziaWG indigo) instead of the globe, and the protocol
pill is gone.

HOW TO TEST (Apple TV)
- Connect WireGuard → you should now have INTERNET (kill switch off by default).
- Connected screen: disc centered, traffic left, details right, no scroll.
- Server cards show the coloured protocol logo. Toggle the kill switch in Settings
  to compare.
