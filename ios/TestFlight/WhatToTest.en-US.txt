v1.1.3.12 (build 83) — IPSec connect fix + VPN cleanup on delete.

Two fixes:

1) IPSEC (IKEv2) SHOULD ACTUALLY CONNECT NOW
   The previous build offered the wrong crypto to the gateway (AES-GCM + DH
   group 19), which the gateway doesn't accept — so IKE silently timed out and
   it failed over to WireGuard. This build matches the gateway's crypto exactly
   (AES-256-CBC + SHA-256 + DH group 14).
   • Pull a fresh IPSec config from the gateway, connect, and confirm it
     ESTABLISHES and stays up (no more instant failover to WireGuard).
   • IMPORTANT: if it STILL fails, please grab a device/Console log
     (process "nesessionmanager" / "NEIKEv2") of one attempt. "no proposal
     chosen" vs "AUTHENTICATION_FAILED / certificate untrusted" tells us
     whether anything remains (the latter would be a server-cert-trust issue
     needing a gateway change).

2) DELETING A CONNECTION NOW REMOVES ITS VPN FROM iOS SETTINGS
   • Delete a connection (or a pool) in the app, then open
     Settings ▸ General ▸ VPN & Device Management ▸ VPN.
   • Confirm the corresponding VPN entry is GONE (it used to orphan there).

REGRESSION CHECK
- WireGuard / AmneziaWG / OpenVPN connect/disconnect unchanged.
- Traffic counter, pools, on-demand rules behave as before.
